The charm system constitutes a multitude of upgrades that are aquired through charm characters([[Fairy Godmother]], [[Peter Pan]], [[Old/Bonds/Snow White|Snow White]], [[The Beauty]], [[White Queen]]) that improves REDs arsenal with powerful upgrades.
You aquire charms everytime you encounter the fairy godmother, you can select from which character you aquire the charm by providing their [[Old/Tesis/Mecánicas/Trinkets|Trinkets]].

The way these upgrades work is a skill tree, once you unlock certain abilities you'll unlock new ones.

There will be two types of charms: Move Upgrades and passives.
